Hex Wargame

Hot-seat, two players, one device. Choose a battle.

How to play

Goal.

Each turn a side does Movement then Combat, then passes the phone.

Move: tap your unit, then tap a highlighted hex. Entering an enemy's surrounding hexes (Zone of Control) stops the unit — and a unit that starts its Movement next to an enemy is locked in place until the fight is decided.

Fight — combat is compulsory: every enemy next to your units must be attacked, and every one of your units next to an enemy must attack. Tap enemies to group them into a battle (a unit touching two enemies attacks both at once), then press Attack and choose which of your units join in. Artillery (A) may also bombard from 2 hexes away — always voluntary, and guns firing from range never suffer losses or retreats. Retreats are one hex; a unit with no safe hex to retreat to is destroyed, and a victor may advance into the hex it clears.

Demoralization: some battles track eliminated strength points per army — reach an army's level and it breaks, ending the game at that instant.

Get around: drag to scroll the map, pinch (or scroll-wheel) to zoom. Fit, top right, shows the whole battlefield at once.

Counters show a letter (I/C/A/G) and CS·MA — combat strength & movement. Artillery adds a third number — its range.

Tap a unit to select it.
