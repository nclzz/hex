Ridge Assault — How to play

Goal. The French (you go first) must capture the Town objective by the end of Turn 6. The Allies win by holding it.

Each turn a side does Movement then Combat, then passes the phone.

Move: tap your unit, then tap a highlighted hex. Entering an enemy's surrounding hexes (Zone of Control) stops the unit.

Fight: in the Combat phase tap an enemy next to your units. All your adjacent units join the attack; odds & a die decide the result.

Counters show a letter (I/C/A/G) and CS·MA — combat strength & movement.
