How to play

Goal.

Each turn a side does Movement then Combat, then passes the phone.

Move: tap your unit, then tap a highlighted hex. Entering an enemy's surrounding hexes (Zone of Control) stops the unit — and a unit that starts its Movement next to an enemy is locked in place until the fight is decided. You may move through friends (never stopping on them). Roads cost ½ MP per hex, streams +2 MP, and rivers can only be crossed at bridges — they also block Zones of Control and combat. Woods are impassable (a road through them is the only way in), and buildings double their defender.

Fight — combat is compulsory: every enemy next to your units must be attacked, and every one of your units next to an enemy must attack. Tap enemies to group them into a battle (a unit touching two enemies attacks both at once), then press Attack and choose which of your units join in. Artillery (A) may also bombard from 2 hexes away — always voluntary, never from inside an enemy Zone of Control, and woods and towns block the line of sight; guns firing from range never suffer losses or retreats. Retreats are one hex: a blocked retreat pushes a friendly unit out of the way (displacement), and only if nobody can make room is the unit destroyed. Whoever clears a hex — attacker or defender — may advance one unit into it. On night turns there is no combat and nobody may enter an enemy Zone of Control.

Demoralization: some battles track eliminated strength points per army — reach an army's level and it breaks, ending the game at that instant.

Get around: drag to scroll the map, pinch (or scroll-wheel) to zoom. Fit, top right, shows the whole battlefield at once.

Inspect: press and hold a hex (right-click on desktop) to see its terrain, its unit and why a unit can't move. Any tap dismisses the panel.

Counters show a letter (I/C/A/G) and CS·MA — combat strength & movement. Artillery adds a third number — its range.

Tap a unit to select it.
